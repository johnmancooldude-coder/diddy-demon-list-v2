DIDDY DEMON LIST

A fully custom Geometry Dash demon list with editable placements, automatic scoring, player profiles, victors, history, stats, and backups.

Important

Videos and thumbnails are optional. Adding a victor does not require a video. A level does not need a thumbnail to appear on the list.
